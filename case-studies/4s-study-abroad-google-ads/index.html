4S Study Abroad on Google Ads: 8,859 enquiries at ₹278

4S Study Abroad is an overseas education consultancy in Dubai, UAE, placing students in Canada, the UK, Germany, Australia and Europe. Over 19 quarters, ₹24.6 lakh of Google Ads brought 8,859 enquiries at ₹277.91 each.

It was not a straight line, and the bad year is on this page too. Every number comes from the Google Ads account.

The challenge

A decision, not a purchase

Nobody buys a degree in one visit. A student fills in a form, a counsellor follows up, and the placement closes offline weeks or months later. The only thing Google Ads can count is the enquiry.

Demand is seasonal around the September and January intakes, every destination is its own auction, and big consultancy networks and universities bid on the same searches.

  • Four destination markets to fill
  • A cost per enquiry the counsellors can convert
  • Through a pandemic and every change to Google's campaign types
Destination first

One campaign set per destination

Instead of one catch-all campaign, each destination got its own campaigns, budgets and bids. Canada, Germany and the UK took 86.5% of spend and brought 91.3% of enquiries.

Germany was the efficiency winner, Canada the volume engine. Generic and university-specific campaigns were the leak: 6% of spend, 2.4% of enquiries, at ₹695 each.

  • Germany: ₹234.01 per enquiry, 8.12% CTR
  • Canada: 38% of enquiries at ₹267.15
  • Generic terms: 2.5 times the average cost
Channels and devices

Search did the work, mostly on phones

Search took 98.66% of spend and brought 98.7% of enquiries. The automated formats ran as small, controlled tests.

Switch between campaign types and devices to see each share.

  • Smart: ₹165.93 per enquiry, the cheapest in the account
  • Performance Max: 11 enquiries, no value signal to learn from
  • Display: zero enquiries, so it was stopped
Five phases

Nineteen quarters, five chapters

Not a straight line: a restart, a scale-up, a peak, a mistake and a rebuild. Pick a year.

Phase 1 · Q2 - Q4 2020

Restart and proof of concept

The account restarted in April 2020, when borders were shut and intakes were being deferred. Spend was raised step by step, from ₹19,854 in Q2 to ₹1,55,688 in Q4, and each step had to keep cost per enquiry in check.

It did better than that: cost per enquiry fell 45.8%, from ₹294.14 to ₹159.43, and conversion rate climbed from 18.80% to 33.14%, the highest of the whole period. Q3 2020 had a 10.07% CTR, also the best on record.

Phase 2 · 2021

Scale-up

The biggest investment year: ₹8.41 lakh, and clicks more than tripled to 18,946. Q3 2021 is still the largest quarter by spend and clicks.

Average CPC fell from ₹61.02 in Q1 to ₹38.67 in Q4, a 36.6% cut, while CTR stayed between 6.7% and 9.1%. Broader demand did dilute intent: conversion rate settled at 14.5% to 17.4% and cost per enquiry rose to ₹280 for the year.

Phase 3 · 2022

The efficiency peak

Spend was cut 29% against 2021, yet cost per enquiry improved 13.4%. Budget moved out of the weakest destination and time-of-day campaigns and into Canada and Germany.

Q3 2022 is the best quarter of the whole period: 1,266 enquiries at ₹157.84 each and a 30.21% conversion rate, on 22.7% less spend than Q3 2021 for 18.3% more enquiries.

What went wrong

Phase 4 · 2023

The reach experiment, and what it cost

Q1 2023 pushed for reach: 1,58,097 impressions, the most of any quarter, on about the same spend as Q1 2022. CTR fell to 2.07%, clicks fell 39%, and cost per enquiry rose to ₹413.

It got worse through the year, to ₹667.41 per enquiry and a 9.66% conversion rate in Q4, the weakest quarter on record. Broad match and audience expansion were bought before the quality signals to control them. Spending into a relevance problem made it bigger.

Phase 5 · 2024

Rebuild and recovery

Spend was cut to its lowest full year, the reach experiment was undone, and budget went back into proven Search campaigns for Canada, Germany and the UK.

Every quality number recovered quarter by quarter: cost per enquiry fell 52.7% from ₹571.11 in Q1 to ₹269.86 in Q4, CPC fell 25.2% and conversion rate rose from 10.98% to 17.37%.

The recovery

From the worst quarter to better than average

The like-for-like test: the same quarter, a year apart. More spend, but far more enquiries for it, and each one cost less than half as much.

  • Enquiries up 303.4%
  • Cost per enquiry down 59.6%
  • Conversion rate back to 17.37%
What the data proves

Six lessons from nineteen quarters

What held true across the whole account, good years and bad.

  • Structure beat budget

    The best quarter, Q3 2022, used 22.7% less money than the biggest-spending one and brought more enquiries at ₹157.84 each.

  • Concentration is correct

    The top 10 campaigns took 84.6% of spend and returned 90% of enquiries. 31 of 76 campaigns were left switched off.

  • Mobile is the market

    78.06% of spend, 78.83% of enquiries, and a cost per enquiry 4.3% below computers.

  • Relevance, not money

    72.76% of searches lost to ad rank and only 15.12% to budget. More budget would not have fixed it.

  • Automation needs a signal

    Smart brought the cheapest enquiries, ₹165.93. Performance Max, with no value data to learn from, cost ₹1,199.39 each.

  • Plan for seasons

    Q3 is the strongest quarter for conversion (25.2% in 2020, 30.2% in 2022). Budgets that build into intakes beat flat monthly pacing.

How we count

Where the numbers come from

Everything is read from the Google Ads account for 1 April 2020 to 31 December 2024, with paused and removed campaigns included. 2020 covers nine months only.

Enquiries are the Google Ads conversions column, mostly lead form submissions. Conversion value was only partly set up, so we do not quote revenue or ROAS. Destination groups come from campaign names.

Common questions

Google Ads for education, answered

What was the cost per lead for this education Google Ads account?

₹277.91 per enquiry across 19 quarters, improving to ₹269.86 in the final quarter, Q4 2024.

How many leads did the campaigns generate?

8,859 enquiries from 50,571 clicks, a 16.47% click-to-enquiry rate.

Which destination market performed best?

Germany, at ₹234.01 per enquiry with an 8.12% CTR, on the second-largest budget.

Does Google Ads work for study-abroad consultancies?

It did here, with the account split by destination and tuned for ad relevance rather than reach. Conversion rate peaked at 33.14% in Q4 2020.

What is the biggest mistake in education PPC?

Buying reach to fix a relevance problem. In 2023 this account had its highest quarterly impressions and, by the end of the year, its worst cost per enquiry.
