4S Study Abroad on Meta: 7,514 student enquiries at AED 8.79

4S Study Abroad is a study-abroad and student visa consultancy in Dubai, UAE. Over 24 months, AED 66.5K of Meta spend brought 5,372 instant form leads and 2,142 WhatsApp conversations.

Every number comes from Meta Ads Manager, and the campaigns are still running. Hover the chart to follow it month by month.

The challenge

Steady student enquiries, at a cost that works

Study abroad is a crowded market in Dubai and across the UAE. Generic "study abroad" ads are expensive and pull in people who are not ready to apply.

4S Study Abroad needed enquiries it could plan around: steady volume for every intake and education expo, at a cost per enquiry a consultancy's margin can carry.

  • Predictable volume for intakes and expos
  • Students and parents, reached separately
  • A cost per enquiry the business can live with
What worked

Four decisions behind the low cost per enquiry

Each one backed by a number from the account.

  • Germany first

    Germany and Schengen intent instead of generic study abroad: AED 10.60 per lead at AED 19.4K of spend in the biggest campaign.

  • Two funnels

    Instant forms and WhatsApp side by side. WhatsApp conversations came in at AED 4.74, about half the cost of a form lead.

  • Creative tested

    Same offer, video against image: AED 11.26 against AED 25.38 per lead. Video won and kept the budget.

  • Events as demand

    Expo RSVP campaigns at AED 0.82 per response on tiny budgets, filling education fairs without touching the lead budget.

How cheap is cheap

About 87% below education benchmarks

Form leads averaged AED 10.41, about USD 2.83. Published Meta benchmarks for education are USD 21.31 (SuperAds) and USD 28.22 (WordStream, 2025).

All 13 UAE lead form campaigns came in under AED 25.38 a lead; 11 of them under AED 16.

  • ~87% below the SuperAds education average
  • ~90% below the WordStream education benchmark
  • Even the most expensive campaign stayed 68% below the SuperAds average
Year on year

Spend went down 20%. Unit costs went down with it.

The second year spent less and got more for each dirham: more WhatsApp campaigns, ABO structures that protected the proven audiences, and video instead of image creative.

  • Cost per form lead: AED 11.27 to AED 9.26
  • Cost per WhatsApp conversation: AED 7.27 to AED 4.18
  • Five more campaigns with delivery
The campaigns

Eight campaigns carried the account

Germany and Schengen campaigns did the heavy lifting, with a separate campaign for parents, who often make the decision.

  • 1,831 leads from Study in Germany
  • 1,306 WhatsApp conversations at AED 3.65
  • Parents targeted on their own
Creative test

Video beat image, 2.3 times over

In the Education Expo 2026 push the same offer ran as two video sets and one image set. The numbers settled it.

  • Video: AED 11.26 and AED 10.63 per lead
  • Image: AED 25.38 per lead
  • 428 of 489 leads came from video

What comes next

Where the account goes from here

Meta does not know who enrolled. These are the next steps we recommend.

  • Close the loop

    Feed CRM outcomes back, so campaigns are judged on applications and enrolments, not just enquiries.

  • Lookalikes of enrolled students

    Build audiences from students who actually enrolled, not from everyone who filled a form.

  • Beyond Germany

    Take what worked for Germany and Schengen to the next destinations.

Common questions

Meta Ads for education, answered

How much does a study-abroad lead cost on Meta Ads in the UAE?

For 4S Study Abroad, instant form leads averaged AED 10.41 (about USD 2.83) over 24 months. Published education benchmarks are USD 21.31 (SuperAds) and USD 28.22 (WordStream, 2025).

Do WhatsApp campaigns work better than lead forms for education?

In this account WhatsApp conversations cost AED 4.74 against AED 10.41 for a form lead, about half. Counsellors also get a warmer start, because the student has already written to them.

How long before Meta Ads give steady enquiry volume?

Here the second year was cheaper than the first: cost per form lead fell 17.8% and cost per WhatsApp conversation 42.5%, on 20% less spend.
