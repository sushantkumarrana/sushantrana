OurKnots on Google Ads: 0.71x to 14x ROAS

Google Ads runs alongside Meta for OurKnots, the fabric laces and trims brand. In February 2026 it spent ₹12K for 6 conversions. In September 2026, on a smaller ₹9K budget, it brought 43.91 conversions at ₹206 each and a 14.05x ROAS.

Every number here comes from the Google Ads account, and the campaigns are still running. Hover the chart to follow the turnaround.

The real struggle

A good first month, then a crash

January looked promising at 5.33x. February did not: spend went up to ₹12K and conversions fell to 6, each costing ₹2,000.

March recovered only to 2.13x, and June slipped back to 2.88x. The turnaround came from making the same budget work harder, not from spending more.

  • Feb 2026: 0.71x ROAS, ₹2,000 per conversion
  • Mar 2026: 2.13x, ₹1,070 per conversion
  • Jun 2026: a dip to 2.88x before the climb
