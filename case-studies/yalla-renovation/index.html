Yalla Renovation does home, villa, apartment, kitchen and bathroom renovation in Dubai. Over 24 months, Search and Performance Max brought 12.5M impressions, 254K clicks and 2,287 tracked form submissions.

The challenge

More renovation leads, without hiding the cost

Renovation leads are expensive, and volume the sales team cannot close is wasted money. Yalla Renovation needed steady enquiries across five services: homes, villas, apartments, kitchens and bathrooms.

Over these 24 months the account reached far more people and recorded far more conversions than in the 24 months before. Spend grew faster than conversions, so each one cost more. Both are on this page.

  • Impressions up 1,298%
  • Recorded conversions up 244%
  • Cost per conversion up 22%, from AED 342 to AED 418
The setup

How the account is built

Search catches people already looking. Performance Max finds more of them. Each service gets its own campaign, so budgets follow what works.

  • Search by service

    Separate Search campaigns for home, bathroom, kitchen, apartment and villa renovation, plus the brand name.

  • Performance Max

    Home, bathroom and kitchen Performance Max campaigns reach people across Google beyond the search results.

  • Store visits

    A Performance Max campaign for store visits, added in 2026 and measured apart from form leads.

  • Conversion tracking

    Lead form submissions are tracked as their own conversion, apart from store visits and WhatsApp clicks.

The hero campaign

One campaign, two thirds of the forms

Home Renovation on Performance Max brought 1,537.49 form submissions and 1,721.49 recorded conversions at AED 221.82 each.

That is 67.2% of all form submissions in the account, on 32.8% of the spend. Pick another campaign to compare.

  • 1,537.49 form submissions
  • AED 221.82 per recorded conversion
  • 67.2% of forms on 32.8% of spend
Devices and networks

Where the conversions actually came from

Spend, clicks and conversions do not line up neatly by device or network, and that is where the next savings are.

Switch between devices and networks to see each share.

  • Mobile: 73.5% of clicks, 67.6% of conversions
  • Computers: 15.2% of clicks, 31.7% of conversions
  • Tablets: 11.3% of clicks, 0.7% of conversions
Search insights

What people searched, and what it cost

The most shown Search ad, in the Home Renovation Search campaign, had 304,659 impressions, 15,425 clicks and a 5.06% click-through rate.

Searches covered the brand name, kitchen, bathroom, apartment and villa renovation in Dubai, and renovation company and contractor terms. Some keywords bring forms cheaply; others are clicked just as often and bring very few.

  • 5.06% CTR on the most shown ad
  • 20.79% CTR on brand searches
  • Broad terms like "renovation dubai" cost the most per form

Common questions

Yalla Renovation on Google Ads, answered

How many leads did Yalla Renovation get from Google Ads?

2,287 tracked form submissions from 1 Oct 2024 to 30 Sep 2026. Google Ads recorded 2,786.8 conversions in total, which also include store visits and WhatsApp clicks.

Which campaign worked best?

Home Renovation on Performance Max: 1,537.49 form submissions, 67.2% of all forms, on 32.8% of the spend, at AED 221.82 per recorded conversion.

Did the cost per conversion go up?

Yes. Against the 24 months before, cost per recorded conversion rose about 22%, from AED 342 to AED 418, while recorded conversions grew 244%.

Why does this case study not show ROAS?

The account does not yet record which leads became jobs, so we do not claim revenue or ROAS.
