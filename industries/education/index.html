The advantage

Why timing your campaigns to admission seasons wins

Education runs on cycles. Demand spikes around exam results, new sessions and intake deadlines, then settles. Many institutes miss this and spend the same all year.

I plan your marketing around these cycles. I build interest before the season, scale up hard during the peak when students are deciding, and capture demand while it is highest. This timing often makes the difference between a full batch and an empty one.

  • Build interest before the season
  • Scale up hard during the peak
  • Capture demand while it is highest

Ads + CRM

Your ads only work properly when your CRM is connected

Google Ads learns from whatever you tell it a conversion is. If the only signal is a form fill, it goes and finds you more form fills, including the wrong numbers and the people who were only browsing.

So we connect your ads to your CRM. Each enquiry is saved with the ad it came from, your counsellors mark which students applied or enrolled, and those admissions flow back into Google Ads as conversions. From then on the platform goes after students who enrol, not just forms.

  • Ad click saved with every student enquiry
  • Counsellors mark applications and admissions in the CRM
  • Admissions sent back to Google Ads
  • Reports on cost per admission

Common questions

Frequently asked questions about education marketing

Channels, seasons, budgets and follow up. If yours is not here, ask me on the call.

How do you get student enquiries for my institute?

I use Google Ads, Meta Ads and local SEO to reach students and parents searching for your courses, then use landing pages and funnels to turn them into enquiries.

Which channels work best for education?

Google Ads and Meta Ads work best, with local SEO adding free reach. Meta is strong for reaching students and parents, and Google captures active course searches.

How much should I spend on marketing?

It depends on your courses, your area and your intake goals. On our call I will suggest a realistic budget so you get enquiries without overspending.

How soon will I see enquiries?

Ads can bring enquiries within days, while SEO builds over a couple of months. Together they give you both fast and lasting results.

Do you do SEO or ads for education?

Both. I combine paid ads with local SEO because together they bring more students than either alone, and I balance them to fit your budget and seasons.

Can you promote a specific course?

Yes. I can build focused campaigns and landing pages for a single course or program, which works very well during admission drives.

Do you build landing pages for my courses?

Yes. I build focused landing pages for each course that turn clicks into enquiries and applications, instead of sending traffic to a busy website.

Can you run admission season campaigns?

Yes. I plan and scale your campaigns around your intake cycles, so you capture the most demand exactly when students are deciding.

Can you connect leads to my CRM?

Yes. I connect enquiries straight into your CRM or system, so your counsellors can follow up quickly, which greatly improves admissions.

Do you set up counselling or webinar funnels?

Yes. I set up funnels for free demos, webinars and counselling sessions, which are some of the best ways to convert students into admissions.

Can you help study abroad consultants?

Yes. I help study abroad and career consultants reach the right students and generate quality enquiries for their programs.

How do you improve lead quality?

I use clear targeting, honest ad messaging and qualifying questions on forms, so you get serious students rather than random clicks.

What will my cost per enquiry be?

It varies by course and area, but my goal is always to lower it over time while keeping quality high. I track it closely so you see the real return.

What reports will I get?

You get clear monthly reports showing enquiries, admissions and cost per enquiry, plus updates on the work done.
