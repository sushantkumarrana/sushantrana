Ads + CRM

Your ads only work properly when your CRM is connected

Google Ads learns from whatever you tell it a conversion is. If the only signal is a call or a form fill, it goes and finds you more of those, including wrong numbers and people who were only asking around.

So we connect your ads to your CRM. Each call and enquiry is saved with the ad it came from, your intake team marks which ones became consultations and signed clients, and those flow back into Google Ads as conversions. From then on the platform goes after real cases, not just enquiries.

  • Ad click saved with every call and enquiry
  • Intake team marks consultations and signed clients in the CRM
  • Signed clients sent back to Google Ads
  • Reports on cost per case

Common questions

Frequently asked questions about law firm marketing

Channels, costs, practice areas and tracking. If yours is not here, ask me on the call.

How do you get clients for my firm?

I use Google Ads, local SEO and practice area landing pages to reach people searching for a lawyer, then turn those searches into calls and enquiries for your firm.

Which channels work best for law firms?

Google Ads and local SEO usually work best, because they reach people actively looking for legal help. Reviews and a strong profile add important trust.

Is legal marketing expensive?

Legal keywords can cost more than average, but a single case is often high value, so the return can be strong. I focus on quality enquiries to keep your cost per case sensible.

How soon will I see results?

Google Ads can bring enquiries within days, while local SEO builds over a couple of months. Together they give you both fast and lasting results.

Do you do legal SEO or ads?

Both. I combine paid ads with local SEO because together they bring more clients than either alone, and I balance them to fit your budget.

Can you target a specific practice area?

Yes. I build focused campaigns and landing pages for each practice area, so you attract the exact type of cases you want.

Can you help me rank on Google Maps?

Yes. I optimize your Google Business Profile and local SEO so your firm shows up in the map results when nearby clients search.

Do you build landing pages for each practice area?

Yes. I build a focused page for each practice area that speaks to that client's problem and turns clicks into enquiries.

How do you track calls and enquiries?

I set up call and intake tracking so we can see exactly which ads and searches are bringing you cases, and improve from there.

How do reviews help my firm?

People rely heavily on reviews when choosing a lawyer. I help you get more genuine reviews and manage your reputation to build trust.

Do you work with solo lawyers?

Yes. I work with solo lawyers as well as larger firms, and I tailor the plan and budget to fit your size and goals.

Can you handle a competitive practice area?

Yes. Competitive areas need sharper targeting and stronger pages, which is exactly where careful marketing makes the biggest difference.

What will my cost per case be?

It varies by practice area and location, but my goal is always to lower it over time while keeping quality high. I track it closely so you see the real return.

What reports will I get?

You get clear monthly reports showing calls, enquiries and cost per case, plus updates on the work done.
