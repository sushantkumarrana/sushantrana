The basics

What Amazon PPC is and why it matters

Amazon PPC, or pay per click advertising, lets you pay to place your products at the top of Amazon search results and on product pages. You only pay when a shopper clicks your ad.

This matters because Amazon is where people go when they are ready to buy, not just browse. Good ads put your product in front of these buyers, win sales, and even help push your product higher in the free organic results over time.

  • Top of search and on product pages
  • Pay only when a shopper clicks
  • Ad sales help your organic rank too

The number that matters

Understanding ACoS and TACoS for real profit

ACoS, or advertising cost of sale, is the share of your ad sales that you spend on ads. A lower ACoS means more profit from each ad sale, so I work hard to keep it healthy for your margins.

TACoS, or total advertising cost of sale, looks at your ad spend against your total sales, including organic. This is the bigger picture number I watch to make sure your ads are truly growing your whole business, not just shifting sales around.

ACoS

Ad spend ÷ ad sales

Lower means more profit from each ad sale.

TACoS

Ad spend ÷ total sales

Includes organic sales. The bigger picture number.

Common questions

Amazon PPC questions, answered

ACoS, targeting, listings and reporting. If yours is not here, ask me on the call.

What is Amazon PPC?

Amazon PPC is pay per click advertising on Amazon. You pay only when a shopper clicks your ad, and your products get shown at the top of search results and on product pages where buyers can see them.

What is ACoS?

ACoS stands for advertising cost of sale. It is the share of your ad sales that you spend on ads. A lower ACoS means you keep more profit from every ad sale.

How much budget do I need for Amazon ads?

It depends on your products, your competition and your goals. Some sellers start small and scale up once we find profitable keywords. On our call I will suggest a realistic starting budget.

What is the difference between Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands?

Sponsored Products promote a single product and drive most ad sales. Sponsored Brands show your logo and several products at the top of search and are great for brand awareness.

Do you optimize my product listings too?

I review your listings and give clear advice on titles, images and content, because even the best ads struggle if the listing is weak. A strong listing plus good ads is what really grows sales.

What is the difference between keyword and product targeting?

Keyword targeting shows your ad when people search certain words. Product targeting places your ad on specific product pages and categories, often your competitors', to win shoppers who are comparing.

How soon will I see results?

You can see clicks and sales within the first few days. The first few weeks are used to gather data and cut waste, so a lower ACoS and steady sales usually build from the first month.

Can you lower my ACoS?

Yes, this is a big part of my job. I do it by adding negative keywords, adjusting bids, improving campaign structure and moving budget to what actually sells.

What is the difference between automatic and manual campaigns?

Automatic campaigns let Amazon find keywords for you and are great for discovery. Manual campaigns give you full control over bids and targeting, which is where the real optimization happens.

What are negative keywords?

Negative keywords are searches you do not want to pay for. Adding them stops wasted clicks and lowers your ACoS, which protects your budget.

Do Amazon ads help my organic ranking?

Yes. More ad sales send a positive signal to Amazon, which can lift your product in the free search results over time. So your ads help even after they stop.

What reports will I get?

You get clear monthly reports showing sales, spend, ACoS, TACoS and your best products, plus updates whenever something important changes.

Can you work with sellers outside India?

Yes. I work with Amazon sellers in the US, Canada and other marketplaces, and I can manage your account from anywhere.

Can you fix my existing Amazon ad account?

Yes. This is a common request. I audit your account, find the waste and rebuild the structure so your ads become profitable again.

Do I need good reviews and a strong listing first?

It helps a lot. Ads bring shoppers, but reviews and a clear listing help turn those clicks into sales. I will point out what to fix first so your ad budget is not wasted.
