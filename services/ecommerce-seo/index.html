The basics

What ecommerce SEO is and why it matters

Ecommerce SEO is the work that helps your online store rank on Google for the searches people use when they want to buy. It focuses on your category pages, product pages and the overall health of your store.

This matters because most shoppers start on Google. If your products show up for buying searches, you get free, high intent traffic that keeps coming, which lowers how much you need to spend on ads.

  • Found for the searches people use to buy
  • Free traffic that keeps coming
  • Less money spent on ads

What I optimize

The ecommerce SEO work I do for you

Seven parts of your store that decide whether shoppers find your products on Google. Pick one to see what it looks like.

The challenge

The special problems of ecommerce SEO and how I solve them

Online stores face SEO problems that normal websites do not. You often have thin or duplicate product descriptions, hundreds of similar pages, filters that create endless URLs, and out of stock products that lose their rankings.

I know how to handle all of these. I write unique content where it matters, control the filter and duplicate page issues, and manage out of stock pages so you do not lose the rankings you worked hard to earn. Solving these problems is what separates real ecommerce SEO from basic SEO.

  • Unique content where it matters
  • Filter and duplicate pages under control
  • Out of stock pages that keep their rankings

Common questions

Ecommerce SEO questions, answered

Products, categories, filters and reporting. If yours is not here, ask me on the call.

What is ecommerce SEO?

Ecommerce SEO is the work that helps your online store rank on Google for buying searches. It focuses on your product pages, category pages and the technical health of your store.

How is ecommerce SEO different from normal SEO?

Ecommerce SEO deals with special problems like hundreds of product pages, duplicate content, filters and out of stock items. It also puts a big focus on category and product pages that capture buyers.

Do I need SEO if I already run paid ads?

Yes. Ads stop when you stop paying, but SEO keeps bringing buyers for free. Using both together usually lowers your overall cost of getting a sale.

Should I optimize category pages or product pages?

Both, but category pages are often the biggest opportunity because they target broad buying searches. I optimize both in the right order for the best gains.

What about duplicate product descriptions?

Duplicate descriptions can hurt your rankings. I write unique content for your most important products and handle the rest in a smart way to avoid problems.

How do you handle out of stock products?

Out of stock pages can lose valuable rankings if handled wrongly. I manage them so you keep the ranking power and guide shoppers to related products.

What is the faceted navigation problem?

Filters on stores can create endless duplicate URLs that confuse Google and waste crawl budget. I control these so your important pages get the attention instead.

How soon will I see results?

Ecommerce SEO usually shows movement over two to three months, with bigger gains building over time as your pages gain authority.

Do reviews help ecommerce SEO?

Yes. Reviews add fresh content and trust, and with review schema your listings can show stars in search, which lifts clicks and sales.

Can you do SEO for my Shopify store?

Yes. I work with Shopify, WooCommerce and other platforms and know the SEO strengths and limits of each one.

Will you write product descriptions?

I write or improve descriptions for your most important products so they are unique and helpful, which supports both rankings and sales.

How do you find product keywords?

I research the exact words shoppers use before buying, including brand, model and buying phrases, and map them to the right pages.

Can ecommerce SEO reduce my ad costs?

Yes. As your organic sales grow, you can rely less on ads for the same revenue, which lowers your overall cost of getting a sale.

Do you fix technical issues too?

Yes. Technical health is a big part of ecommerce SEO. I fix crawl, speed and duplicate content issues that hold stores back.

What reports will I get?

You get clear monthly reports showing rankings, organic traffic and sales, plus updates on the work done.
