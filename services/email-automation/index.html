The basics

What email automation is and why it matters

Email automation is a set of emails that send themselves based on what a person does, such as signing up, buying, or leaving items in a cart. Once it is built, it runs around the clock without you touching it.

This matters because manual follow up never happens consistently. Leads go cold and sales are lost. Automation makes sure every lead and customer gets the right emails at the right time, every single time, which brings you more sales from the audience you already have.

  • Emails that send themselves
  • Triggered by what each person does
  • Running around the clock

Why it works

Why email automation is so powerful

Email sells to people who already know you. Automation makes sure it happens every time.

  • It nurtures leads automatically

    Most leads are not ready to buy right away. Automation keeps warming them up until they are, without you chasing them.

  • It recovers lost sales

    Automated emails bring back people who abandoned a cart or went quiet, recovering sales you would otherwise lose.

  • It builds relationships at scale

    You can stay in touch with thousands of people in a personal way, all automatically, which builds trust and repeat business.

  • It delivers a high return

    Email is known for one of the best returns of any channel, because it sells to people who already know you, at almost no cost per email.

What I set up

The email flows I build for you

Each flow handles one moment in your customer's journey. I set up the ones that fit your business. Pick one to see what it looks like.

Welcome sequences. The first emails a new lead gets, which build trust and start moving them towards their first purchase.

Lead nurture sequences. A series that educates and warms up leads over time, so they are ready to buy when the moment comes.

Abandoned cart recovery. Emails that bring back shoppers who left items in their cart, which recovers a large share of lost sales.

Post purchase and onboarding. Emails after a purchase that improve the customer experience and encourage repeat orders and reviews.

Re engagement and win back. Emails that wake up quiet subscribers and bring lapsed customers back to buy again.

Sales and promo sequences. Timed email campaigns for your launches, offers and seasonal promotions that drive a burst of sales.

Segmentation. I organize your list into groups so each person gets emails that actually fit them, which greatly lifts your results.

The real value

Why the right message at the right time wins

Sending everyone the same email at the same time does not work well. What works is sending the right message based on what each person has actually done.

That is exactly what automation does. Someone who just signed up gets a welcome. Someone who left a cart gets a reminder. Someone who has gone quiet gets a win back. Each person gets the email that fits their moment, which feels personal and drives far more sales than a plain newsletter ever could.

  • Signs up: a welcome
  • Leaves a cart: a reminder
  • Buys: a thank you and a review request
  • Goes quiet: a win back

Common questions

Frequently asked questions about email automation

Flows, tools, spam, results and reporting. If yours is not here, ask me on the call.

What is email automation?

Email automation is a set of emails that send themselves based on what a person does, like signing up or leaving a cart. Once built, it runs around the clock without you touching it.

How is automation different from a newsletter?

A newsletter is one email sent to everyone at once. Automation sends the right email to each person based on their actions, which feels personal and drives far more sales.

Do I need email automation?

If you collect leads or sell online, yes. Automation follows up consistently, which manual effort never does, so it recovers sales you would otherwise lose.

What are the most important email flows?

The key ones are a welcome sequence, a lead nurture sequence, abandoned cart recovery, post purchase emails and a win back sequence. I set up the ones that fit your business.

Can you set up abandoned cart emails?

Yes. Abandoned cart emails bring back shoppers who left items behind, and they recover a large share of otherwise lost sales for online stores.

Do you write the emails?

Yes. I write emails that sound like you and are built to build trust and drive action, not just fill space in an inbox.

Which email tools do you use?

I work with popular tools like Mailchimp, Klaviyo and others, as well as email inside GoHighLevel or HubSpot. I will use the one that fits your business best.

Can you connect it to my store or CRM?

Yes. I connect your automation to your store or CRM, so it reacts to real customer actions like purchases and sign ups.

How soon will I see results?

Some flows, like abandoned cart emails, can start recovering sales within days. Others build value steadily as more people move through them.

Is email marketing still worth it?

Yes. Email consistently delivers one of the best returns of any channel, because it sells to people who already know you at almost no cost per email.

How do you avoid landing in spam?

I follow good practices for setup, list health and content, and I keep your emails relevant, which all help your emails reach the inbox rather than the spam folder.

Can you set up segmentation?

Yes. I organize your list into groups so each person gets emails that fit them, which greatly improves your open rates and sales.

How do you measure success?

I track opens, clicks and, most importantly, sales from your emails, so we can see the real value and keep improving your flows.

What reports will I get?

You get clear reporting on how your flows are performing, including opens, clicks and revenue, plus updates on the improvements made.
