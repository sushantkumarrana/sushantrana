The basics

What Google Ads can do for your business

Google Ads puts your business in front of people at the exact moment they are searching for your product or service. This is the most powerful type of buyer intent, because the person is already looking for what you offer.

Unlike SEO, which builds over months, Google Ads can start bringing traffic and leads on the same day your campaign goes live. This makes it perfect for new offers, launches and any business that needs customers now.

  • Shown only to people searching for what you sell
  • Live and bringing traffic on day one
  • Every click, lead and sale tracked

Why it works

Why Google Ads is one of the best ways to grow

Google Ads is popular for a reason. When it is managed well, it gives you fast, clear and profitable results.

  • You reach high intent buyers

    Your ads only show to people who are actively searching for what you sell. These are warm buyers, not random visitors, which means better quality leads.

  • You stay in control of the budget

    You decide how much to spend per day and per month. I make sure that budget goes only into the keywords and audiences that bring results.

  • You only pay for clicks

    In most campaigns you pay only when someone clicks your ad, not when it is shown. This keeps your money focused on real interest.

  • Everything is measurable

    You will always know your cost per click, your cost per lead and your return on ad spend. There is no guessing, only clear numbers.

The real problem

Why most Google Ads accounts waste money

Many business owners tell me their Google Ads just do not work. In most cases the account was set up in a rush and never fixed. The most common leaks are broken conversion tracking, no negative keywords, the wrong keyword match types and sending clicks to a weak page.

When I take over an account, I fix these problems first. Very often this alone brings down the cost per lead and starts turning a losing account into a profitable one.

Ads + CRM

Google Ads only works properly when your CRM is connected

Google Ads learns from whatever you tell it a conversion is. If the only signal is a form fill, it goes and finds you more form fills, including the fake numbers and the people who were only browsing.

So on every account we connect the ads to your CRM. The click is saved with each lead, your sales team updates the lead status, and the qualified leads and sales flow back into Google Ads as conversions, with their value. From then on Google bids for buyers, not for forms.

  • Click ID saved with every lead
  • Lead status synced from Zoho, HubSpot or GoHighLevel
  • Qualified leads and sales sent back as conversions
  • Reports on cost per qualified lead and cost per sale

Common questions

Google Ads management questions, answered

Budgets, timelines, tracking and reporting. If yours is not here, ask me on the call.

What are Google Ads?

Google Ads is Google's paid advertising system. It lets your business appear at the top of Google search results, on YouTube, on Maps and across millions of websites, so you reach people who are looking for what you sell.

How much budget do I need for Google Ads?

It depends on your industry and your goals. Some clients start small and scale up once we see profit. On our first call I will suggest a realistic starting budget for your market so you never overspend.

How soon will I get leads from Google Ads?

Google Ads can bring traffic and leads within the first few days. The first few weeks are used to gather data and improve the account, so stable results usually build from the first month onwards.

What is the difference between Google Ads and SEO?

Google Ads brings instant paid traffic, while SEO builds free traffic slowly over time. Ads are great for fast results, and the best growth usually comes from using both together.

What is Quality Score and why does it matter?

Quality Score is Google's rating of how relevant your ad, keyword and landing page are. A higher score lowers your cost per click and improves your ad position, so it saves you money.

What are negative keywords?

Negative keywords are searches you do not want to pay for. Adding them stops your ads from showing on the wrong searches, which saves a large part of your budget.

Do you set up conversion tracking?

Yes, and it is one of the first things I fix. Without correct tracking you cannot know which clicks turn into leads and sales, so I set this up properly using tools like Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics 4.

What is Performance Max?

Performance Max is a smart Google campaign that shows your ads across all of Google from one setup. It works well when it is guided with strong data and clear goals, which is exactly what I do.

Do I need a CRM to run Google Ads?

You can run ads without one, but then Google only learns from form fills, good and bad alike. When your CRM sends back which leads were qualified and which became sales, Google Ads bids for more people like your real customers. I connect Zoho, HubSpot or GoHighLevel to your account, or set one up if you do not have a CRM yet.

Can you run Google Shopping ads for my store?

Yes. I set up your product feed and run Shopping and Performance Max campaigns that put your products in front of ready to buy shoppers and track every sale.

Do I need a landing page for Google Ads?

In most cases a focused landing page converts much better than a normal website page. If your page is weak I will point it out and help you improve it, because it directly affects your cost per lead.

Will you write the ad copy?

Yes. I write clear, benefit driven ad copy and test different versions to find the ones that bring the most clicks and conversions at the lowest cost.

How do you stop my budget from being wasted?

I add negative keywords, use the right match types, fix tracking, pause weak keywords and send traffic only to strong pages. These steps cut waste and lower your cost per result.

Can you fix my existing Google Ads account?

Yes. This is one of the most common requests I get. I audit your account, find the leaks and rebuild the parts that are holding you back.

Do you manage Google Ads for local businesses?

Yes. Local businesses do very well on Google Ads when campaigns are set up carefully with the right locations, keywords and call tracking.

What reports will I get?

You get clear monthly reports in plain language that show what you spent and what you earned back, plus updates whenever something important changes.
