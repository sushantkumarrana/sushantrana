The basics

What Google Shopping Ads are and how they sell

Google Shopping Ads are product ads that appear at the top of Google search with an image, a price and your store name. They are powered by two tools working together, Google Merchant Center, which holds your product information, and Google Ads, which runs the campaigns.

Because the shopper sees the product and price before clicking, the clicks you get are more likely to turn into sales. This makes Shopping one of the highest value channels for any online store.

  • Image, price and store name in search
  • Merchant Center plus Google Ads
  • Clicks from shoppers who already know the price

Why it works

Why Google Shopping Ads are great for ecommerce

Shopping puts the product itself in front of the shopper. Here is why that works so well for online stores.

  • Your products are seen, not just read

    Shopping Ads show a real photo of your product. People shop with their eyes, so a clear image gets far more attention than a plain text ad.

  • High buyer intent

    Shopping Ads appear when people are searching for a product to buy. These are warm shoppers who are close to a purchase.

  • The price is shown upfront

    Because the price is visible before the click, the people who click are already comfortable with your pricing. This means better quality clicks and less wasted spend.

  • Strong return for online stores

    When the feed and campaigns are set up well, Shopping often gives one of the best returns on ad spend for ecommerce.

How it works

How Google Shopping actually works

Shopping Ads do not use keywords the way Search Ads do. Instead, Google reads your product feed, which is a file that lists all your products with their titles, images, prices and details. Google then decides which searches to show your products for, based on that feed.

This is why the feed is the real engine behind Shopping. A clean, well written feed leads to better placement and cheaper sales. A messy feed holds your whole account back, no matter how much you spend.

  • No keywords, the feed decides
  • Titles, images, prices and details
  • A clean feed means better placement
The secret

Why the product feed is the most important part

Most Shopping accounts fail because of a weak product feed, not a weak budget. If your product titles are unclear or your images are poor, Google shows your products for the wrong searches and your sales suffer.

I spend real time getting your feed right. This means clear, keyword rich titles, correct product types, strong images and complete details. A strong feed is the single biggest thing that lifts your Shopping results.

Common questions

Google Shopping Ads questions, answered

Merchant Center, feeds, budgets and reporting. If yours is not here, ask me on the call.

What are Google Shopping Ads?

Google Shopping Ads are product ads that appear in Google search with an image, price and store name. They help online stores reach shoppers who are ready to buy.

What is Google Merchant Center?

Google Merchant Center is the tool that stores all your product information, like titles, prices and images. Google reads this to decide when and where to show your Shopping Ads.

Do I need a product feed?

Yes. The product feed is the file that lists all your products and their details. It is the engine behind Shopping Ads, and I set it up and optimize it for you.

How are Shopping Ads different from Search Ads?

Search Ads are text ads that use keywords. Shopping Ads use your product feed and show an image and price. Shopping is usually better for selling products, while Search is better for services.

How much budget do I need for Shopping Ads?

It depends on your products and competition. Some stores start small and scale up once we find winning products. On our call I will suggest a realistic starting budget.

Why is the product feed so important?

Google uses your feed to decide which searches show your products. Clear titles and good images bring better placement and cheaper sales, while a weak feed holds your account back.

What is the difference between Standard Shopping and Performance Max?

Standard Shopping gives you more control over products and bids. Performance Max is a smart campaign that reaches across all of Google from one setup. I choose the right mix for your goals.

How soon will I see results?

Shopping Ads can bring traffic and sales within the first few days. The first few weeks are used to gather data and cut waste, so strong results usually build from the first month.

Can you fix my disapproved products?

Yes. Product disapprovals are common and can quietly kill your sales. I find the cause, fix the feed or settings and get your products approved and selling again.

Do you optimize my product titles and images?

Yes. I improve your titles, product types and image quality inside the feed, because these directly affect which searches show your products and how many people click.

What is ROAS for Shopping Ads?

ROAS, or return on ad spend, tells you how much revenue you earn for every rupee spent. For Shopping, a strong ROAS is one of the clearest signs your store is growing profitably.

Can you handle a store with many products?

Yes. I structure large catalogs in a smart way so we can control spend and push budget to your best sellers, even with hundreds of products.

Do you work with Shopify and WooCommerce stores?

Yes. I work with Shopify, WooCommerce and other platforms, and I can connect your store to Merchant Center using the right feed method.

What reports will I get?

You get clear monthly reports showing sales, spend, return and your best products, plus updates whenever something important changes.

Can you fix my existing Shopping account?

Yes. I audit your Merchant Center and campaigns, fix the feed and rebuild the structure so your Shopping Ads become profitable again.
