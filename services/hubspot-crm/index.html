The basics

What HubSpot is and why it matters

HubSpot is an all in one platform that brings your CRM, marketing, sales and customer service together in one place. Its core CRM is free, and it grows with powerful tools as your needs grow.

This matters because most businesses run their marketing and sales in separate tools that do not talk to each other. Leads fall through the gaps. HubSpot connects everything, so a lead is tracked from the first click all the way to a closed deal, with nothing lost in between.

  • CRM, marketing, sales and service in one place
  • A free core CRM to start with
  • Every lead tracked from first click to closed deal

Why HubSpot

Why HubSpot is a great choice

HubSpot is built to keep marketing and sales on the same page. Here is why it is a strong fit.

  • A powerful free CRM to start

    HubSpot's core CRM is free, so you can start organizing your leads and sales without a big upfront cost, then add tools as you grow.

  • Marketing and sales in one place

    Your ads, forms, emails, pipeline and deals all live together, so your marketing and sales teams finally work from the same information.

  • Strong automation and workflows

    HubSpot can automate follow ups, tasks and emails with easy to build workflows, which saves hours and stops leads going cold.

  • Easy to use and scale

    HubSpot is known for being user friendly, and it scales from a small team to a large operation without needing to switch systems.

The real value

Why aligning marketing and sales wins more deals

The biggest reason leads are lost is that marketing and sales are not connected. Marketing brings in a lead, sales never hears about it in time, and the chance is gone.

HubSpot closes that gap. When it is set up right, every lead flows from marketing straight to sales with the full history attached, and automation makes sure it is followed up fast. This alignment often brings more deals from the leads you already have, which is where the real value lies.

Ads + CRM

Your ads only work properly when your CRM is connected

Google Ads learns from whatever you tell it a conversion is. If the only signal is a form fill, it goes and finds you more form fills, including the fake numbers and the people who were only browsing.

HubSpot already knows which of those leads became real deals. So when we set it up, the ad click is saved on every contact, your team moves deals through the pipeline as they work them, and HubSpot sends the qualified leads and closed deals back to Google Ads as conversions, with their value. From then on your ads go after buyers, not forms.

  • Ad click saved on every HubSpot contact
  • Deal stages kept up to date by your sales team
  • Qualified leads and closed deals sent back to Google Ads
  • Reports on cost per qualified lead and cost per deal

What is HubSpot?

HubSpot is an all in one platform that brings your CRM, marketing, sales and service together. It keeps all your leads and customers in one place and helps you manage them from first contact to closed deal.

Is HubSpot free?

HubSpot's core CRM is free, so you can start without a big cost. It also offers paid tools with more power, and I will help you choose only what you actually need.

Is HubSpot good for small business?

Yes. You can start with the free CRM and add tools as you grow. It is user friendly and scales well, which makes it a strong fit for small and growing businesses.

Can you set it up for me?

Yes. I handle the full setup, from onboarding and pipelines to automation and training, so you get a working system without the headache of doing it yourself.

Can you design pipelines and deal stages?

Can you connect my website forms?

Can you automate follow ups with workflows?

Yes. I build workflows that automate follow ups, tasks and reminders, so leads are always handled on time and nothing goes cold.

Can you set up email sequences?

Yes. I set up email and sales sequences so your prospects get timely, consistent follow up without you doing it by hand.

Can you migrate my data?

Yes. I move your existing leads and contacts into HubSpot cleanly and safely, so you start with all your data in one place.
