The basics

What a landing page is and why it matters

A landing page is a single, focused page built for one goal, such as getting a lead, a call or a sale. Unlike a normal website page, it removes distractions and guides the visitor towards one clear action.

This matters because focus wins. When a visitor lands on a page with one message and one offer, they are far more likely to act than on a busy page with many links and choices. This is why landing pages convert so much better.

  • One message that matches your ad
  • One offer, one action
  • Every lead and sale tracked

The secret

What actually makes a landing page convert

A landing page is not just a pretty design. It converts because of a few key things working together. A clear headline that matches what the visitor expects, one single goal, a strong offer, trust signals, fast loading and a simple form.

I build every one of these into your page. I focus on the message and the flow, not just the looks, because that is what actually turns visitors into customers. A beautiful page that does not convert is a waste, and I will not build you one.

Landing page questions, answered

Copy, tracking, testing and timelines. If yours is not here, ask me on the call.

What is a landing page?

A landing page is a single, focused page built for one goal, like getting a lead or a sale. It removes distractions and guides the visitor towards one clear action.

How is a landing page different from my homepage?

Your homepage has many links and goals. A landing page has just one message and one action, which is why it converts far better for ads and offers.

Why do I need a separate landing page for my ads?

Sending ad traffic to your homepage wastes clicks. A dedicated landing page matches your ad and focuses the visitor, so you get more leads and sales from the same budget.

What makes a landing page convert?

A clear headline, one single goal, a strong offer, trust signals, fast loading and a simple form. I build all of these into your page.

How long does it take to build?

Most landing pages take a few days to a couple of weeks depending on the design and copy. I will give you a clear timeline once I know your needs.

Will my landing page be mobile friendly?

Yes. Most traffic comes from phones, so every page I build is designed to look and work perfectly on mobile.

Do you write the copy?

Yes. I write conversion focused copy, because the words and the message are what really drive results, not just the design.

Do you set up tracking?

Yes. I set up conversion tracking so every lead and sale from the page is counted correctly, which lets us measure and improve results.

Can you A/B test the page?

Yes. I build your page so we can test different headlines, offers and layouts and keep lifting your conversion rate over time.

How soon will I see results?

A landing page can start converting the moment your traffic hits it. From there, testing helps push your results higher over the following weeks.

Can you connect it to my CRM or email tool?

Yes. I can connect your form to your CRM, email tool or spreadsheet, so leads flow straight into your system for follow up.

Can you build on WordPress, Shopify or a page builder?

Yes. I can build your landing page on WordPress, Shopify or a dedicated landing page tool, depending on what suits you best.

Will the page load fast?

Yes. Speed is vital for conversions, so I build your page to load quickly, which also helps with ad quality and rankings.
