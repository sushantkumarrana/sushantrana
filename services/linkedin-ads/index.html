The basics

What LinkedIn Ads are and why they are different

LinkedIn Ads are paid ads shown to professionals on the LinkedIn platform. What makes LinkedIn special is that people list their real job title, company, industry and skills, so you can target buyers by exactly what they do for work.

This is something no other platform can match. If your ideal customer is a specific type of business owner, manager or decision maker, LinkedIn lets you put your message right in front of them.

  • Target by real job title, company and industry
  • Reach the people who approve budgets
  • Built for B2B buying

Why it works

Why LinkedIn Ads are perfect for B2B

When you sell to other businesses, LinkedIn gives you something no other platform does: the buyer's real professional details.

  • You reach real decision makers

    You can target the exact people who approve budgets and make buying decisions, instead of hoping your message reaches them.

  • Precise professional targeting

    Target by job title, company, industry, company size and seniority. This level of business targeting is unique to LinkedIn.

  • Higher quality leads

    The leads you get from LinkedIn are often more serious and better qualified, because they come from a professional setting.

  • Great for high value deals

    If one new client is worth a large amount to you, LinkedIn is often the smartest place to spend your ad budget.

Ad types

The LinkedIn ad types I run for you

Each format does a different job, from the feed to the inbox. I pick the ones that fit your offer and your buyer. Pick one to see what it looks like.

Sponsored Content. Single image or video ads that appear in the LinkedIn feed.

The most common and flexible format for reaching your audience.

Carousel ads. Multiple swipeable cards in one ad.

Great for telling a story or showing several benefits or services.

Lead Gen Forms. Forms that fill in automatically with the person's LinkedIn details. This makes it very easy for busy professionals to enquire.

Lifts your lead numbers.

Message ads. Ads delivered straight to the LinkedIn inbox of your target audience.

Useful for direct, personal offers and invites.

Text and dynamic ads. Smaller ads that appear on the side of LinkedIn.

A low cost way to stay visible to your audience.

Honest advice

Why LinkedIn costs more but can still be worth it

LinkedIn clicks and leads cost more than most other platforms. Many people see this and stop. That is a mistake for the right business.

The reason is simple. If one new client is worth a large amount to you, then paying more for a well targeted lead still gives you a strong return. I will always give you honest advice on whether LinkedIn is the right fit for your business and your budget.

Ads + CRM

LinkedIn Ads only works properly when your CRM is connected

LinkedIn Ads learns from whatever you tell it a conversion is. If the only signal is a form fill, it goes and finds you more form fills, including the fake numbers and the people who were only browsing.

So on every account we connect the ads to your CRM. Each lead is saved with where it came from, your sales team updates the lead status, and the qualified leads and sales flow back into LinkedIn Ads as conversions, with their value. From then on the platform goes after buyers, not forms.

  • Ad click saved with every lead
  • Lead status synced from Zoho, HubSpot or GoHighLevel
  • Qualified leads and sales sent back as conversions
  • Reports on cost per qualified lead and cost per sale

Common questions

LinkedIn Ads questions, answered

Cost, targeting, lead quality and reporting. If yours is not here, ask me on the call.

What are LinkedIn Ads?

LinkedIn Ads are paid ads shown to professionals on LinkedIn. Because people share their real job details, you can target buyers by their role, company and industry, which is ideal for B2B.

Are LinkedIn Ads good for B2B?

Yes. LinkedIn is the best platform for B2B, because you can reach the exact decision makers who make buying choices, which you cannot do as precisely anywhere else.

Why are LinkedIn Ads more expensive than other platforms?

You are paying to reach high value professionals in a business setting. The cost per lead is higher, but the lead quality is often much higher too, so the return can still be strong.

How much budget do I need for LinkedIn Ads?

LinkedIn needs a higher budget than Facebook or Google to gather enough data. On our call I will give you an honest view of the budget you need to see real results.

What is a Lead Gen Form?

A Lead Gen Form is a LinkedIn ad form that fills in automatically with the person's professional details. It makes enquiring quick and easy, which brings you more leads.

Can you target specific job titles and companies?

Yes. I can target by job title, function, seniority, company, industry and company size, so your ads reach exactly the right people.

How soon will I see leads?

You can start getting leads in the first couple of weeks. Because B2B buying takes longer, I focus on lead quality and steady results rather than instant volume.

Are LinkedIn Ads good for small businesses?

They can be, if you sell to other businesses and your average deal is valuable. For low value or B2C products, other platforms are usually a better fit, and I will tell you honestly.

Do you create the ad creatives and copy?

Yes. I write the copy, craft the offer and guide the creatives so your ads speak clearly to busy professionals.

Can LinkedIn Ads help with hiring?

Yes. LinkedIn is excellent for reaching candidates with specific skills and roles, so it works well for recruitment and hiring campaigns.

What is Sponsored Content?

Sponsored Content is an ad that appears directly in the LinkedIn feed as a single image, video or carousel. It is the most common and flexible LinkedIn ad format.

How do you measure success on LinkedIn Ads?

I track leads, cost per lead and lead quality using conversion tracking, and I focus on the leads that actually turn into real business for you.

Can you run LinkedIn Ads for clients outside India?

Yes. I work with clients in the US, Canada and other countries and can target any professional audience you want to reach.

What reports will I get?

You get clear monthly reports in plain language showing spend, leads, cost per lead and results, plus updates on anything important.
