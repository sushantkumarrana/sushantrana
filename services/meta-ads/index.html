The basics

What Meta Ads are and why they matter

Meta Ads are the paid ads you see on Facebook and Instagram. Both platforms are owned by Meta and share one powerful ad manager, so a single strategy can reach billions of active users.

The real power of Meta is its targeting. You can show your ads to people based on their age, location, interests, behaviour and past actions. This means your message reaches the people most likely to buy, not random viewers.

  • Facebook and Instagram from one ad manager
  • Targeting by age, location, interests and behaviour
  • Reach people who already know you

Why Meta Ads work so well for business growth

When Meta Ads are set up correctly, they can bring a steady flow of leads and sales at a low cost. Here is why so many businesses rely on them.

  • Huge active audience

    Billions of people use Facebook and Instagram every day. Your customers are already there, spending hours scrolling, which gives you plenty of chances to reach them.

  • Powerful, precise targeting

    I can target people by interests, behaviour, location and life events, and even reach people who look like your best customers. This keeps your money focused on the right audience.

  • Great for leads and sales

    Meta Ads work for both service businesses that need leads and online stores that need sales. The same system can do both when the strategy is right.

  • Visual and video friendly

    Images, videos, reels and carousels grab attention fast. This makes Meta perfect for showing off your product and building trust quickly.

Placements

Where your Meta Ads will appear

Meta shows your ads in many places across Facebook and Instagram. I choose the best placements for your goals and let the system spend where it performs best. Pick one to see it on a phone.

Facebook and Instagram feeds. Your ads appear as people scroll through their main feed, which is where most attention and action happens.

Ad types

The Meta ad types I run for you

Different goals need different ad formats. I plan and build the right ones for your business. Pick one to see what it looks like.

Image ads. Simple, clean single image ads that deliver a strong message fast.

Great for offers and clear promotions.

Video ads. Short videos that tell your story, show your product and build trust.

Video is one of the strongest formats on Meta.

Carousel ads. Multiple images or products in one swipeable ad.

Perfect for showing a range of products or steps.

Lead form ads. Ads that collect a name, email and phone number right inside Facebook or Instagram, without the person leaving the app.

Excellent for service businesses.

Catalog and collection ads. Product ads pulled from your store catalog that show the right products to the right shoppers.

Ideal for ecommerce.

Retargeting ads. Ads that follow up with people who visited your site or engaged with your page but did not buy.

One of the cheapest ways to win back sales.

The secret

Why the creative is the most important part on Meta

On Meta, the image or video you use matters more than almost anything else. A boring ad will fail even with perfect targeting, while a strong scroll stopping ad can bring cheap leads and sales.

That is why I put so much care into your creatives and copy. I plan a clear message, guide the images and videos, and test many versions to find the ones that truly work. This is often the difference between ads that lose money and ads that make it.

  • A clear message planned first
  • Images and videos guided, not left to chance
  • Many versions tested to find the winners
Ads + CRM

Meta Ads only works properly when your CRM is connected

Meta Ads learns from whatever you tell it a conversion is. If the only signal is a form fill, it goes and finds you more form fills, including the fake numbers and the people who were only browsing.

So on every account we connect the ads to your CRM. Each lead is saved with where it came from, your sales team updates the lead status, and the qualified leads and sales flow back into Meta Ads as conversions, with their value. From then on the platform goes after buyers, not forms.

  • Ad click saved with every lead
  • Lead status synced from Zoho, HubSpot or GoHighLevel
  • Qualified leads and sales sent back as conversions
  • Reports on cost per qualified lead and cost per sale

Common questions

Meta Ads questions, answered

Budgets, the Meta Pixel, creatives and reporting. If yours is not here, ask me on the call.

What are Meta Ads?

Meta Ads are the paid ads that run on Facebook and Instagram. Both platforms share one ad system called Meta, so a single strategy can reach people across both.

What is the difference between boosting a post and running proper Meta Ads?

Boosting is a simple, limited option that mostly buys likes. Proper Meta Ads use full targeting, tracking and creative testing to bring real leads and sales. The results are very different.

How much budget do I need for Facebook and Instagram ads?

It depends on your goals and your industry. Some clients start small and scale up once we see profit. On our call I will suggest a realistic starting budget for your market.

How soon will I see results?

Meta Ads can start bringing leads within the first few days. The first two to four weeks are used to test and learn, so stable results usually build from the first month.

What is the Meta Pixel and why do I need it?

The Meta Pixel is a small piece of code on your website that tracks what visitors do. It lets Meta learn who your buyers are and lets you retarget people, which greatly improves your results.

Can Meta Ads work for ecommerce?

Yes, very well. I use catalog ads, retargeting and smart audiences to bring ready to buy shoppers to your products and track every sale.

Can Meta Ads get me leads for a service business?

Yes. Lead form ads and strong landing pages can bring a steady flow of enquiries for service businesses, often at a low cost per lead.

Do you create the images and videos?

I guide and direct your creatives and write the copy. I plan exactly what the images and videos should show, and I can work with your designer or with simple assets you already have.

What is retargeting on Meta?

Retargeting shows ads to people who already visited your site or engaged with your page but did not buy. It is one of the cheapest and most profitable types of Meta Ads.

Why did my previous Facebook ads not work?

Usually it is one of three reasons: weak creatives, wrong targeting or broken tracking. I check all three, fix the real problem and rebuild the account the right way.

Which is better for my business, Facebook or Instagram?

Most businesses do best using both, since they share one system. I let the data decide where your budget performs best, rather than guessing.

Do you write the ad copy?

Yes. I write clear, benefit driven copy and test different versions to find the ones that bring the most action.

How do you target the right audience?

I use interests, behaviour, location and lookalike audiences based on your best customers. Then I test and refine until we find the audiences that convert.

Can you run lead form ads?

Yes. Lead form ads collect contact details right inside Facebook and Instagram, which makes it easy for people to enquire and easy for you to follow up.

What reports do I get?

You get clear monthly reports in plain language showing spend, leads, sales and return, plus updates whenever something important changes.
