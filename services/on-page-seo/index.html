The basics

What on-page SEO is and why it matters

On-page SEO is the work you do on your own pages to help them rank, such as the content, the titles, the headings, the images and the internal links. It tells Google exactly what your page is about and why it deserves to rank.

This matters because even a great page will struggle if Google cannot clearly understand it. Good on-page SEO connects your page to the searches your customers use, which lifts your rankings and your traffic.

  • Every page matched to the right search
  • Titles people want to click
  • Content that fully answers the searcher

Why it works

Why on-page SEO gives fast wins

It is the part of SEO you control completely, which is why it so often moves first.

  • It tells Google what your page is about

    Clear titles, headings and content help Google match your page to the right searches. This is the base of every ranking.

  • You are in full control

    Unlike links, which depend on others, on-page SEO is fully in your hands. That means faster changes and faster results.

  • It lifts rankings for your keywords

    Tuning your pages for the right keywords and search intent often moves you up the results without needing anything else.

  • Better pages convert more

    Good on-page work also improves the experience for visitors, so more of them turn into leads and sales.

What I optimize

The on-page elements I optimize for you

Seven parts of every page that decide how well it ranks. Pick one to see what the work looks like.

Keyword research and mapping. I find the right keywords for each page and map them so every page targets clear, non competing searches.

Title tags and meta descriptions. I write titles and descriptions that include your keywords and make people want to click from the search results.

Headings and structure. I set up a clean H1 and H2 structure so both Google and readers can follow your page easily.

Content optimization. I improve your content so it fully answers what the searcher wants, which is what Google rewards most.

Internal linking. I link your pages together in a smart way, which spreads authority and helps Google find and rank your important pages.

Image and URL optimization. I optimize your images, alt text and page URLs so nothing on the page is left holding back your rankings.

Schema markup. I add structured data so Google understands your pages better and can show rich results and AI answers.

The real key

Why matching search intent beats stuffing keywords

Old SEO was about repeating a keyword many times. That does not work today and can even hurt you. What Google really wants is a page that fully answers what the searcher is looking for.

So when I optimize a page, I focus on search intent, which means understanding what the person actually wants and making sure your page gives it clearly. This is the difference between a page that ranks and one that gets ignored.

  • What the searcher actually wants
  • Answered clearly on the page
  • Keywords used naturally, not stuffed

Common questions

On-page SEO questions, answered

Titles, intent, content and reporting. If yours is not here, ask me on the call.

What is on-page SEO?

On-page SEO is the work done on your own pages to help them rank, such as the content, titles, headings, images and internal links. It tells Google what your page is about.

What is the difference between on-page and off-page SEO?

On-page SEO is about your own pages and content. Off-page SEO is about signals from outside your site, mainly backlinks. Both are needed for strong rankings.

What is the difference between on-page and technical SEO?

On-page SEO focuses on content and page elements. Technical SEO focuses on the health of your site, like speed and indexing. They work together as a complete SEO plan.

What are title tags and meta descriptions?

The title tag is the clickable headline shown in search results, and the meta description is the short summary below it. Good ones include your keyword and encourage people to click.

How important are headings for SEO?

Very important. A clear H1 and H2 structure helps Google understand your page and helps readers follow it. Good headings often improve both rankings and time on page.

What is keyword mapping?

Keyword mapping means giving each page its own clear keyword and search intent, so your pages do not compete with each other and each one targets the right searches.

What is search intent?

Search intent is what the person really wants when they search. Matching your page to that intent is the most important part of ranking today, more than repeating keywords.

How does internal linking help?

Internal links connect your pages and pass authority between them. They help Google find your important pages and can lift the rankings of the pages you link to.

Do you rewrite my content?

I improve and expand your content where needed so it fully answers the searcher and includes the right keywords naturally. I keep your voice and message intact.

How soon will I see results?

On-page changes often show movement within a few weeks, which makes it one of the faster parts of SEO. Bigger gains build over a couple of months.

What is image alt text?

Alt text is a short description of an image that helps Google understand it and helps accessibility. It is a small but useful on-page ranking signal.

Do you add schema markup?

Yes. I add structured data so Google understands your pages better and can show rich results, which also helps your content appear in AI answers.

How is on-page SEO different from writing blogs?

Blogs are new content you create. On-page SEO is optimizing any page, new or old, so it ranks. I often improve your existing pages first, since that brings quick wins.

Can on-page SEO fix a page that dropped in rankings?

Often yes. A page can drop when its content no longer matches what searchers want. I refresh and re-optimize it to win back its position.

What reports will I get?

You get clear reports showing rankings, traffic and the on-page work done, in plain language, plus updates on progress.
