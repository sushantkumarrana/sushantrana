The basics

What a sales funnel is and why it matters

A sales funnel is a guided journey that takes a stranger and turns them into a customer, one step at a time. Instead of dropping people on a busy website, it leads them through a clear path towards one action.

This matters because a normal website lets people wander and leave. A funnel focuses them. Each step warms them up, builds trust and removes doubts, so far more of your visitors end up buying instead of bouncing away.

  • One clear path towards one action
  • Details captured before people leave
  • Follow up that warms visitors into buyers

Why you need one

Why a sales funnel beats a normal website

A website shows people around. A funnel walks them to the sale. Here is the difference.

  • It stops leaking leads

    A funnel captures contact details early and follows up, so the people who are not ready to buy today are not lost forever.

  • It guides buyers step by step

    Instead of leaving people to figure it out, a funnel leads them through a clear path that makes buying easy.

  • It automates the selling

    With follow up built in, a funnel keeps selling for you around the clock, even while you sleep.

  • It makes revenue predictable

    When you know how many visitors turn into customers, you can scale your traffic with confidence and grow on purpose.

What I build

The sales funnels I build for you

Each funnel type does a different job. I choose the right one for your offer and your goal. Pick one to see what it looks like.

Lead generation funnels. Funnels that capture leads with a strong offer, then nurture them into enquiries and sales.

The stages

How each stage of your funnel is built to convert

A good funnel guides people through clear stages. First it grabs attention and creates awareness. Then it builds interest with a helpful offer. Next it drives the decision by removing doubts and showing value. Finally it makes taking action simple and easy.

I design each stage on purpose, with the right page, message and follow up at every step. Nothing is left to chance. This is why a proper funnel converts so much better than just sending traffic to a page and hoping.

  • Awareness: grab attention
  • Interest: a helpful offer
  • Decision: remove doubts, show value
  • Action: simple and easy

Common questions

Frequently asked questions about sales funnels

Funnel types, copy, tools, timelines and tracking. If yours is not here, ask me on the call.

What is a sales funnel?

A sales funnel is a guided journey that turns a stranger into a customer, one step at a time. Each step is designed to move the visitor closer to buying.

How is a funnel different from a website?

A website lets people wander and often leave. A funnel focuses them on one path and one action, which is why it converts far more of your traffic into sales.

Do I really need a funnel?

If you want to sell something specific and get more from your traffic, yes. A funnel captures leads and follows up, so you stop losing people who were not ready to buy today.

What types of funnels are there?

Common types include lead generation, webinar, sales, application, ecommerce and tripwire funnels. I choose the right type for your offer and your goal.

How long does it take to build?

Most funnels take from one to a few weeks depending on the number of pages and the automation. I will give you a clear timeline once I know your offer.

Do you write the copy?

Yes. I write conversion focused copy for your funnel, because the message and the offer are what really drive the results.

Do you set up the automation and email follow up?

Yes. Follow up is where a lot of sales happen. I set up the email and message sequences that keep working on leads who did not buy right away.

Which tools do you build funnels on?

I can build on GoHighLevel, WordPress or a dedicated funnel tool, depending on what suits your business and budget best.

Can you connect it to my CRM?

Yes. I connect your funnel to your CRM or system, so leads flow in automatically and your follow up stays organized.

How soon will I see results?

A funnel can start converting as soon as traffic hits it. From there, testing and follow up help lift your results over the following weeks.

Can you A/B test the funnel?

Yes. I set your funnel up so we can test different offers, headlines and pages, and keep improving your conversion rate over time.

Do you set up tracking?

Yes. I set up tracking so we can see exactly where people drop off and where the funnel is winning, then improve from there.

Can a funnel work for my business?

Most businesses with a clear offer can benefit from a funnel. On our call I will tell you honestly whether a funnel is right for you and which type fits best.

What reports will I get?

You get clear reporting on visitors, conversions and where people drop off, so you can see exactly how your funnel is performing.
