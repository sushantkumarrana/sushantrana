The basics

What a website SEO audit is and what it finds

A website SEO audit is a full health check of your site's search performance. It looks at the technical side, the content, the structure, the links and how you compare to your competitors.

The goal is simple. It finds the exact reasons your site is not ranking as well as it should, and it gives you a clear list of what to fix, in the right order.

What it covers

What my website SEO audit covers

Seven areas, each checked by hand. Pick one to see the kind of thing the audit finds.

Technical health. I check crawling, indexing, site speed and Core Web Vitals, the technical issues that quietly block rankings.

On page SEO. I review your titles, headings, content and keywords to see where your pages are weak or missing the mark.

Site structure and links. I look at how your pages are organized and linked, since this affects how well authority flows through your site.

Backlink profile. I review the links pointing to your site to see if they are helping you or holding you back.

Content gaps. I find the topics and keywords your competitors rank for that you are missing, which are your biggest opportunities.

Competitor comparison. I compare your site to your top competitors so you can see exactly where you are behind and how to catch up.

Mobile and local. I check your mobile experience and, if relevant, your local SEO and Google Business Profile.

Common questions

Website SEO audit questions, answered

What it covers, how long it takes and what happens after. If yours is not here, ask me on the call.

What is a website SEO audit?

A website SEO audit is a full health check of your site's search performance. It finds the reasons your site is not ranking well and gives you a clear plan of what to fix.

Is the audit really free?

Yes, the audit is free with no obligation. I offer it because it is a genuine way for you to see the value I can bring, whether or not you decide to work with me.

How long does the audit take?

It usually takes me a few days to run a proper audit and prepare your report, because I review it by hand rather than sending an instant automated scan.

What does the audit cover?

It covers your technical health, on page SEO, site structure, backlinks, content gaps, competitor comparison and your mobile and local presence.

Will you tell me why I am not ranking?

Yes. That is the whole point. I find the real reasons your site is held back and explain them clearly, along with what to fix first.

Do I have to buy anything after the audit?

No. There is no obligation. You are free to use the action plan yourself. If you want my help to fix things, we can talk, but there is no pressure.

What do I need to give you?

Just your website address and a little about your business and goals. If needed, I may ask for access to your Search Console or analytics for a deeper look.

How is this different from automated audit tools?

Automated tools spit out generic data and often miss the real issues. My audit adds a real expert's judgement, so you get insight and priorities, not just numbers.

Will you fix the issues too?

The audit itself shows you what to fix. If you want me to carry out the fixes, I offer that as a separate service, and we can discuss it after you see the report.

Can you audit an ecommerce site?

Yes. I audit online stores too, including the special issues stores face like duplicate content, filters and product page problems.

Do you check my backlinks?

Yes. I review your backlink profile to see whether your links are helping you or holding you back, and whether any harmful links need attention.

Do you compare me to competitors?

Yes. I compare your site to your top competitors so you can see exactly where you are behind and where your best opportunities are.

How detailed is the report?

It is detailed enough to be useful but written in plain language, with the main issues and a prioritized action plan you can actually follow.

Can this help if my site was doing well but dropped?

Yes. An audit is one of the best ways to find out why a site lost rankings, so you can fix the cause and recover.
