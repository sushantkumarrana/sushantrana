Common questions

Shopify store development questions, answered

Timelines, pricing, ownership, migrations and support. If yours is not here, ask me on the call.

How long does a Shopify store build take?

Most builds take four to six weeks: discovery in week one, design by week two, development through week five, then testing and launch in week six. You get a dated schedule before anything is agreed.

What does the price cover?

Scoping, design, hand written development, two revision rounds, the responsive build, browser and device testing, and thirty days of support after launch. Nothing is added to the invoice later.

Who owns the code and the design files?

You do, completely. Source files, design assets and the theme are handed over when the project closes. There is no licence to renew and no vendor to stay tied to.

How many revision rounds are included?

Two rounds come as standard. Further rounds are available at a flat hourly rate, though most projects are settled after the first.

What if I need changes once the store is live?

The first thirty days cover fixes and small adjustments at no cost. Beyond that we work either on a monthly retainer or task by task, whichever suits you.

How does payment work?

Half to begin and half on delivery is the usual arrangement. Longer projects can be split across milestones instead. Bank transfer and UPI both work for clients in India.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. Send yours across before we talk specifics, or use ours if that is easier.

Can I see examples of stores you have built?

Yes. On the discovery call we walk you through relevant projects and live links, so you can judge the work rather than take our word for it.

How quickly do you respond to support requests?

You will hear back within a few working hours, and most issues are resolved inside a day or two. Anything breaking on a live storefront is handled the same day.

Will my existing apps still work?

Yes. The usual ecommerce stack, including Klaviyo, ReCharge, Yotpo, Judge.me, Gorgias and the common shipping tools, is carried across and reconfigured properly.

What if I am unhappy with the result?

Then it is not finished. We would rather return to the drawing board than hand over a storefront you would hesitate to share.

Do you handle putting the store live?

Yes. The new theme goes up as a duplicate for review first, then goes live while you watch, and we stay reachable for the following day.

How will we communicate during the project?

Short written updates on WhatsApp or Slack, a call whenever a decision needs one, and one shared document holding the brief, the mockups and every decision made.

What do you need from me to start?

Your current store link if there is one, any brand guidelines, a few reference sites you admire, and a short note on what the store has to achieve. A structured questionnaire follows kickoff.

Do you offer ongoing support after the first month?

Yes. Monthly retainers cover continued optimisation, design updates, app work and fixes for store owners who would rather not manage it themselves.

Will my store look different from other Shopify sites?

Yes. Nothing is reused between clients. Wireframes start from your catalogue and your customers, so the finished storefront belongs to your brand alone. Why most Shopify stores look alike, and how to avoid it

Can I edit the content myself afterwards?

Yes. Every section is exposed in the Shopify theme editor, so wording, images, colours and buttons can all be changed without touching code.

What if I want to add products or new sections later?

The store is built on Online Store 2.0 with reusable sections, so you can add products, collections and pages yourself. Bring us back only when you want something genuinely new built.

Can you move my products across from another platform?

Yes. Products, customer records, order history, redirects and the SEO groundwork all come across, which is what keeps your existing rankings intact through the move.

Which apps will I actually need?

Only the ones that earn their place. We suggest a short, deliberate stack for reviews, email and retention, set it up correctly, and leave the rest out so the store stays fast.

Ready to build your Shopify store?

Tell me what you sell and where you want the business to be in a year. I will come back with a plan, a price and a launch date.
