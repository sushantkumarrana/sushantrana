The basics

What technical SEO is and why it matters

Technical SEO is the work that helps search engines crawl your site, understand your pages and rank them without any blocks. It covers things like site speed, mobile friendliness, site structure, indexing and code level details.

This matters because Google cannot rank what it cannot properly reach or understand. Fixing technical issues often unlocks rankings that your content already deserves but was not getting.

  • Google can reach every important page
  • Pages stored and ready to show in search
  • A fast, clean site for people and Google

Why it works

Why technical SEO is so important

Everything else in SEO sits on top of a healthy site. When the base is right, the rest pays off.

  • No rankings without crawling and indexing

    If Google cannot crawl and index your pages, they simply will not show in search. Fixing this is the first step to any rankings at all.

  • Speed affects rankings and sales

    A slow site loses both rankings and customers. Faster pages rank better and keep more visitors, which means more leads and sales.

  • Mobile first is the standard

    Google mainly looks at the mobile version of your site. If your site works poorly on phones, your rankings suffer everywhere.

  • It powers all your other SEO

    Content and links work far better on a clean, fast site. Technical SEO is the base that makes everything else pay off.

What I fix

The technical issues I check and fix

Eight areas that decide whether Google can reach, read and rank your site. Pick one to see what the fix looks like.

Crawling and indexing. I make sure Google can find and index your important pages, and I stop it from wasting time on pages that do not matter.

Site speed and Core Web Vitals. I improve your loading speed and Core Web Vitals, which are the speed and stability scores Google uses when ranking your site.

Mobile friendliness. I make sure your site looks and works well on phones, since that is what Google mainly judges.

Site structure and internal links. I organize your pages and internal links so both users and Google can move through your site easily.

Sitemap and robots file. I set up a clean XML sitemap and a correct robots file, so Google knows what to crawl and what to skip.

Fixing errors. I fix broken links, wrong redirects and duplicate content that quietly drag down your rankings.

Security and canonicals. I make sure your site uses secure HTTPS and correct canonical tags, so there is no confusion about which page should rank.

The speed factor

Why Core Web Vitals and speed matter so much

Core Web Vitals are Google's way of measuring how fast and stable your pages feel to a real user. They look at how quickly your page loads, how fast it responds and whether things jump around while loading.

A slow, jumpy site frustrates visitors and loses rankings and sales. I improve these scores by fixing heavy images, slow code and other issues, which makes your site faster for people and stronger in search.

  • Heavy images made lighter
  • Slow code cleaned up
  • Layout that stays still while loading

Common questions

Technical SEO questions, answered

Audits, speed, indexing and reporting. If yours is not here, ask me on the call.

What is technical SEO?

Technical SEO is the work that helps search engines crawl, understand and rank your website. It covers things like site speed, mobile friendliness, indexing, site structure and code level details.

How is technical SEO different from on-page SEO?

On-page SEO is about your content, keywords and page structure. Technical SEO is about the health of your site itself, making sure Google can reach and read it. Both are needed to rank well.

What is a technical SEO audit?

A technical SEO audit is a deep check of your website to find every issue affecting your crawling, indexing, speed and rankings. It gives a clear picture of what is holding your site back.

What are Core Web Vitals?

Core Web Vitals are Google's scores for how fast and stable your pages feel to a real user. They look at load speed, response time and layout stability, and they affect your rankings.

Why is my site not ranking even with good content?

Often a hidden technical issue is the cause, like slow speed, crawl problems or indexing errors. Good content cannot rank if Google cannot properly reach or understand it.

How does site speed affect SEO?

A slow site loses rankings and visitors. Google prefers fast pages, and users leave slow ones, so improving speed helps both your rankings and your sales.

What are crawling and indexing?

Crawling is when Google reads your pages, and indexing is when it stores them to show in search. If either fails, your pages will not appear in results at all.

What is schema markup?

Schema markup is special code that helps Google understand your pages better. It can lead to rich results in search and also helps your content show up in AI answers.

Do you fix the issues or just find them?

I fix them. Many services only hand you a report. I go further and make the actual changes, so your site really improves.

How long does a technical audit take?

A full audit usually takes a few days, depending on the size of your site. I then share the findings and a clear plan sorted by priority.

What tools do you use?

I use trusted tools like Google Search Console, PageSpeed Insights and professional crawlers to find issues, but the real value is in knowing which fixes actually move rankings.

Can technical SEO help my ecommerce store?

Yes, a lot. Large stores often have crawl, speed and duplicate content issues. Fixing these can unlock rankings and sales across many product pages at once.

Do I need technical SEO if my site is small?

Even small sites can have speed, mobile or indexing issues that quietly hurt rankings. A quick audit will show whether it is worth the effort for your site.

Will you break my site while fixing it?

No. I work carefully and test every change. My goal is to improve your site safely, and I always check that everything still works after each fix.

What reports will I get?

You get a clear audit report and simple updates showing what was fixed and the impact, all in plain language.
