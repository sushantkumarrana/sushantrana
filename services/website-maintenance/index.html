The basics

What is website maintenance?

Website maintenance is the regular work that keeps a live website secure, fast and correct: backups, software and plugin updates, security checks, bug fixes, broken link repairs and content changes. It is to a website what servicing is to a car.

Why does my website need a maintenance plan?

Software, plugins, browsers and search engines keep changing. Without regular care a site slows down, breaks or becomes an easy target for hackers. A plan keeps it updated, backed up and working, with someone already responsible when something goes wrong.

What is included in website maintenance services?

Every plan includes backups, security scans, disaster recovery, hosting and webmail support, SSL and domain monitoring, and broken link fixes. Higher plans add CMS and plugin updates, content and design changes, new pages, Search Console and Analytics setup, and malware cleanup.

What happens if I do not maintain my website?

Outdated plugins and themes are the most common way sites get hacked. Unmaintained sites also slow down, show errors after browser or platform updates, lose forms and payments without anyone noticing, and slowly drop in Google rankings.

How often should a website be maintained?

Backups and security checks should run at least monthly, and weekly for busy or ecommerce sites. Plugin and platform updates are best applied as they release, after testing. A full health check of speed, SEO and broken links every month keeps small problems small.

Can I maintain my website myself?

You can handle simple text changes yourself. Updates, backups, security and fixing what breaks after an update need technical knowledge and time. Most business owners find a plan costs less than the hours, and the risk, of doing it alone.

What is the difference between website maintenance and website support?

Maintenance is planned, preventive work like updates, backups and checks. Support is fixing a problem after it appears. Every plan here covers both, so you do not pay separately when something breaks.

Cost and plans

How much does website maintenance cost in India?

Plans start at ₹18,000 a year for 7 hours of work a month, with a 15 hour plan at ₹22,000 and a 30 hour plan at ₹44,000 a year. Clients in India pay 18% GST on top. Very large or complex sites may need a custom quote.

How much does website maintenance cost per month?

Billed yearly, the plans work out to roughly ₹1,500, ₹1,830 and ₹3,670 a month, depending on how many hours of work your site needs each month.

What is a website AMC?

AMC stands for Annual Maintenance Contract. It is a yearly agreement where one team takes care of your website's upkeep for a fixed price, instead of you paying for each fix separately. All three plans here are AMCs.

Which plan should I choose?

A small business site with few changes is usually fine on Essential. If you update content often or run a CMS with many plugins or apps, pick Growth. Ecommerce stores and high-traffic sites suit Complete.

Are hosting and domain charges included?

No. Hosting and domain renewals are paid to your provider and stay in your name. The plan covers managing them: tracking renewal dates, handling hosting issues with the provider and keeping SSL and webmail working.

Platforms

Which platforms and CMS do you maintain?

WordPress, WooCommerce, Shopify, Wix, Wix Studio, Squarespace, Webflow, Framer, Magento, BigCommerce, Joomla, Drupal, Ghost and HubSpot CMS, plus custom HTML, React and Next.js sites and sites built with AI tools.

Do Wix, Squarespace, Shopify or Webflow websites need maintenance?

Yes, though less of it. The platform takes care of hosting and core security, but content, SEO settings, speed, forms, integrations, apps, domains and broken links are still yours to look after. Those are exactly what the plans cover.

What does WordPress maintenance include?

Core, theme and plugin updates tested before they go live, PHP version checks, database cleanup, backups, malware scanning, speed work and fixing anything an update breaks. WordPress needs the most regular care of any CMS because of its plugins.

What does Shopify store maintenance include?

Theme updates, app reviews and leftover app code cleanup, product and collection uploads, speed work, broken link and image fixes, checkout and payment checks, and design changes to sections and pages.

Do you maintain Webflow and Framer websites?

Yes. That covers CMS collection updates, new pages built with your existing components, interaction and layout fixes on new screen sizes, form and integration checks, SEO settings and publishing changes safely.

Do you maintain custom HTML, React or vibe-coded websites?

Yes. Sites built by hand or with AI tools such as Lovable, Bolt, v0, Replit or Cursor still need dependency and security updates, bug fixes, deployment and hosting care, and someone who can read and safely change the code.

Can you move my website to another platform?

Yes. Moving between platforms, for example Wix to WordPress or WooCommerce to Shopify, is a separate project. Tell me on the call and I will quote it alongside the maintenance plan.

Security, speed and SEO

What if my site gets hacked or goes down?

The site is restored from the latest clean backup, the cause is found and fixed, and security is tightened so it does not happen again. Disaster recovery is part of every plan.

How often is my website backed up?

Monthly on Essential, twice a month on Growth and weekly on Complete. Hosted platforms like Shopify and Wix are backed up at the content level where the platform allows it.

Can you make my slow website faster?

Yes. Speed work covers image compression, removing unused plugins, apps and scripts, caching, and fixing whatever the Core Web Vitals report flags. Keeping it fast after that is part of the monthly checks.

Does website maintenance help SEO?

Yes. Google favours fast, secure, error-free sites. Fixing broken links and 404 errors, keeping SSL valid, improving speed and watching Search Console for problems all protect the rankings you already have.
