Why WordPress

Why WordPress is a smart choice for your website

Flexible, owner friendly and great for SEO, which is why so much of the web runs on it.

  • You own and control it

    Your site is fully yours. You are not locked into a closed platform, and you can move or change it whenever you want.

  • Easy to update yourself

    You can edit content, add pages and update images on your own, which saves you time and money over the long run.

  • Great for SEO

    WordPress is built in a way that search engines love, which gives your site a strong base to rank well from day one.

  • Endless flexibility

    With the right setup and plugins, WordPress can do almost anything, from bookings to online stores to memberships.

  • It grows with you

    As your business grows, your site can grow too, adding new features and pages without starting over.

Common questions

WordPress development questions, answered

Timelines, editing, hosting and what happens after launch. If yours is not here, ask me on the call.

Why should I choose WordPress?

WordPress is flexible, easy to manage and great for SEO. You fully own your site, you can update it yourself, and it can grow with your business over time.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. I build your site so you can edit content, add pages and update images on your own. I also show you how during the handover.

How long does it take to build a website?

It depends on the size and features, but most business websites take a few weeks. I will give you a clear timeline once I understand your needs.

Will my website be mobile friendly?

Yes. Every site I build works well on phones, tablets and computers, since most visitors and Google both look at the mobile version first.

Will my website be fast?

Yes. I build speed into the site from the start, because a fast site keeps visitors happy and ranks better on Google.

Is WordPress good for SEO?

Yes. WordPress has a strong SEO foundation, and I set up your site the right way so it is ready to rank from day one.

Do you use Elementor or custom code?

I use the right tool for your needs. Page builders like Elementor make it easy for you to edit later, while custom work is used where it adds real value.

Can you build an online store?

Yes. I build online stores on WordPress using WooCommerce, with products, payments and a smooth checkout that is designed to sell.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. I can give your old site a fresh, modern redesign that improves both its look and its results, while keeping what already works.

Do you provide the content?

I can guide your content and help shape it, and I can arrange writing if needed. Good content matters a lot, so I make sure your pages are clear and helpful.

What about hosting and domain?

I will guide you on choosing good hosting and a domain, and I can handle the setup for you so everything is done properly.

Will you train me to use the site?

Yes. During handover I show you how to manage your site, so you feel confident making updates on your own.

Do you provide maintenance after launch?

Yes. I offer website maintenance as a separate service to keep your site updated, secure and running smoothly after launch.

Is WordPress secure?

WordPress is secure when it is set up and maintained properly. I follow good practices during the build and can keep it secure with ongoing maintenance.

What do I get when the site is done?

You get a complete, working website that you own and can manage, along with a handover so you know how to use it.
