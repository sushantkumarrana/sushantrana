The basics

What YouTube Ads are and why they matter

YouTube Ads are video and image ads that run on YouTube. They are managed through Google Ads, which means they come with the same powerful targeting and tracking.

Billions of hours of video are watched on YouTube every day. This gives your business a huge, engaged audience and one of the cheapest ways to get in front of people using video.

  • Run through Google Ads, with its targeting and tracking
  • A huge, engaged audience
  • One of the cheapest ways to reach people with video

Why it works

Why YouTube Ads are so powerful

Video reaches people while they watch content they love, and with Google behind it, every view can be targeted and tracked.

  • A massive audience

    Almost everyone watches YouTube. Whatever your business, your customers are spending time there every single day.

  • Cheap views

    You can often reach thousands of viewers for a small budget. This makes YouTube one of the most cost effective ways to build awareness.

  • Powerful targeting

    Because YouTube runs on Google Ads, you can target by interests, keywords, topics and even people who visited your site.

  • Video builds trust fast

    People trust what they can see and hear. Video lets you show your product, your face and your story, which builds trust far quicker than text.

Ad formats

The YouTube ad formats I run for you

Each format suits a different goal, from a six second reminder to a full story. I pick the mix that fits your message. Pick one to see what it looks like.

Skippable in stream ads. Ads that play before or during a video and can be skipped after a few seconds.

You mostly pay only when someone watches, so it is very cost effective.

Non skippable ads. Short ads that must be watched fully.

Best when you want to be sure your full message is seen.

Bumper ads. Six second ads that deliver a quick, punchy message.

Great for staying in people's minds and reinforcing your brand.

In feed video ads. Ads that appear in YouTube search results and next to related videos.

Perfect for reaching people who are actively looking.

Shorts ads. Ads that appear inside YouTube Shorts.

Short video is growing fast here, and attention is high.

How I reach the right viewers

Views only matter when they come from the right people. I combine three kinds of targeting so your video reaches people who are likely to care about your offer. Pick one to see how it works.

The advantage

Why video builds trust faster than any other ad

Text and images can tell people about your business, but video lets them see and feel it. When a person watches you explain your product, hears your voice and sees real results, trust builds much faster.

This is why YouTube Ads are so strong for growing a brand and warming up buyers. Even people who do not buy right away will remember you, and remarketing brings them back when they are ready.

Ads + CRM

YouTube Ads only works properly when your CRM is connected

YouTube Ads learns from whatever you tell it a conversion is. If the only signal is a form fill, it goes and finds you more form fills, including the fake numbers and the people who were only browsing.

So on every account we connect the ads to your CRM. Each lead is saved with where it came from, your sales team updates the lead status, and the qualified leads and sales flow back into YouTube Ads as conversions, with their value. From then on the platform goes after buyers, not forms.

  • Ad click saved with every lead
  • Lead status synced from Zoho, HubSpot or GoHighLevel
  • Qualified leads and sales sent back as conversions
  • Reports on cost per qualified lead and cost per sale

Common questions

YouTube Ads questions, answered

Cost, formats, videos and targeting. If yours is not here, ask me on the call.

What are YouTube Ads?

YouTube Ads are video and image ads that run on YouTube. They are managed through Google Ads, so they come with strong targeting and full tracking.

How much do YouTube Ads cost?

YouTube is one of the cheaper platforms for reach, and you can often get thousands of views for a small budget. On our call I will suggest a realistic starting budget for your goals.

Do I need a professional video?

Not always. A clear, honest video often works better than a fancy one. I guide the script and message, and I can work with a simple video or advise you on creating one.

How am I charged, per view or per click?

It depends on the format. With skippable ads you usually pay only when someone watches a good part of your ad or clicks, which keeps your money focused on real interest.

What is a skippable in stream ad?

It is an ad that plays before or during a video and can be skipped after a few seconds. You mostly pay only when someone keeps watching, so it is very cost effective.

What are bumper ads?

Bumper ads are six second ads that cannot be skipped. They are great for quick, memorable messages that keep your brand in people's minds.

Can YouTube Ads get me sales or only views?

They can get you sales when set up with proper tracking and remarketing. Views are only the start, and my job is to turn attention into real customers.

How do you target the right viewers?

I use interests, topics, keywords, placements, custom audiences and remarketing, so your ads reach people who are likely to care about your offer.

Can I retarget people who visited my site?

Yes. Remarketing on YouTube shows your ads to people who already visited your website, which is one of the cheapest ways to bring back lost buyers.

How soon will I see results?

Awareness and views build quickly, often within days. Leads and sales usually build over the first few weeks as we test and improve the account.

Are YouTube Ads good for small businesses?

Yes. Because reach is cheap, even small businesses can build strong awareness on YouTube when the targeting and message are right.

Do you help with the video script?

Yes. I guide the script and message so your video holds attention and drives action. A good message matters more than expensive production.

Can you run YouTube Ads for ecommerce?

Yes. I use video and remarketing to build interest and bring shoppers back to your products, with full tracking of sales.

What reports will I get?

You get clear monthly reports in plain language showing spend, views, actions and results, plus updates on anything important.
